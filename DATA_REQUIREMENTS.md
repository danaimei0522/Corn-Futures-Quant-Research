# Data requirements and third-party terms

No third-party data or empirical result is included. The underlying study used Dalian corn futures information obtained through AKShare interfaces, including Sina Finance, and consulted public Dalian Commodity Exchange materials. Public accessibility or retrieval through a library is not a redistribution license.

AKShare’s [project statement](https://github.com/akfamily/akshare#statement) describes its data as for academic research/reference and acknowledges upstream providers; it does not grant rights on behalf of those upstream providers. The [Sina Finance User Agreement](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2021-05-12/detail-ikmxzfmm2033220.shtml) reserves rights in service content and describes restrictions on unauthorized copying, dissemination, and providing content to third parties. The project has no source-specific written permission or contract for publishing DCE data either.

Before full analytical reproduction, acquire appropriate user-level access to actual-contract daily OHLCV/open-interest history, a point-in-time contract listing/expiry roster, exchange calendar, route/index panels, and documented missing-date masks. Confirm separately whether your agreement permits publishing any inputs or data-derived tables, metrics, visualizations, reports, or model artifacts. This public package intentionally omits all such deliverables until that permission is documented.

Never add API response payloads, licensed market data, account details, credentials, or local forward-feed snapshots. The 2019-04-22 historical gap remains unresolved in the private research dataset; do not impute it.
